---
title: "UK Government announce intention to regulate beekeepers"
pubDate: 2026-11-21
author: "Jackie Thomas"
---

As part of the new trading agreement with the EU, the UK Government has just announced their intention to regulate hobbyist  beekeepers, as part of an overall regulation of smallholders and animal keepers.

Changes will not come into effect until 1st March 2028 and further detail is to follow.

The draft guidance can be read on the Gov.uk website [Sanitary and Phytosantitary (SPS) Agreement  Guidance: Smallholders and animal keepers: preparing for changes](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/smallholders-and-animal-keepers-preparing-for-changes)

This guidance is meant for smallholders and animal keepers, who keep farm animals on a small holding, as a hobby, for leisure, for household use or as pets. Beekeepers are specifically mentioned, along with other more typical animals such as sheep, cattle etc.

For bks it will be administered through through Beebase. 

For most beekeepers who are already registered and follow good practice with regards to recording keeping, it appears that they may be few changes – though movement of bees, (including queens and breeding material), and the sale of bees, may become more regulated.

With Tropilaelaps and Small hive beetle knocking on our frontier and our bee inspector time stretched, these regulations should be seen in a positive light. Hopefully, the expectation on commercials will be as or more exacting, for as we know it is through the importation of bees and queens that the greatest risk lies. However, as hobbyists drive this importation trade the answer to this is in our own hands!
